    What the 5 points mean
    Every pair starts at 3 (balanced), then moves up or down by up to 1 point for each of three checks:
    1 · Moon-sign rapport — how the two Moon signs sit relative to each other (Bhakoot): supportive +1, neutral 0, tense (6/8 or 2/12 axis) −1
    2 · Moon-lord friendship — are the planets ruling each Moon sign friends (+1), neutral (0) or enemies (−1)
    3 · Ascendant-lord friendship — same test on the rulers of each Lagna, showing how their core approach to life fits
    5 Excellent · 4 Good · 3 Balanced · 2 Needs effort · 1 Challenging. Hover any cell for its exact breakdown.
    About the method & limits. Positions use real ephemeris data (Astronomy Engine) converted to the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa (linear approximation, accurate to about an arc-minute). Rahu/Ketu use the mean lunar node. Houses follow the traditional whole-sign system. House strength (++/+/–/––), planet colouring, aspects and the Mahadasha/Antardasha/Pratyantardasha dates are all generated from classical rules of thumb (house lordship, kendra/trikona/dusthana/upachaya placement, exaltation/debilitation, own-sign status, graha drishti, and proportional Vimshottari subdivision) — a simplified, rules-based reading, not a substitute for a full classical analysis (no divisional charts, no Shadbala six-fold strength, no complete Ashtakoot Guna Milan) or for professional, medical, financial or legal advice. Birth time accuracy matters most for the Ascendant, houses and dasha dates — a few minutes' error can shift things.
