Privacy Policy

Kutumb Kundali (“the app”) — last updated October 2026.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What you enter

To draw charts you type names, relations, dates, times and places of birth for yourself and family members. This information is saved only in your device’s local storage inside the app. It is never uploaded to any server owned by us, and we have no accounts, no sign-in and no way to see it.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No account creation
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise device location (birth places are chosen from a built-in list or typed in by you)

Third-party content

When you are online, the app may download its fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive standard connection information (such as your IP address) as part of serving those font files. Chart calculations run entirely on your device.

Deleting your data

Remove any person with the ✕ button inside the app, or clear the app’s storage / uninstall the app to erase everything.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Nature of the content

Astrological readings are generated from traditional rules for cultural and entertainment purposes. They are not medical, financial, legal or professional advice.
